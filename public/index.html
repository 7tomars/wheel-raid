How it works

Squeekchain settles a round every 10 minutes. Wheel Raid reads the public round work from api.squeekchain.com/v1/core and converts it to distance (1 work = 8 m).

Every round you call RUN (distance ≥ median of the last 10 rounds) or SLEEP (below). Correct call = 1.5× damage. A correct healer lifts the whole team by 10%.

Boss HP is shared by everyone. Kill it and the next tier spawns immediately — same week, bigger HP, same leaderboard.

Recaps are posted to the community Discord channel by webhook. No private keys, no transactions, no real tokens.
